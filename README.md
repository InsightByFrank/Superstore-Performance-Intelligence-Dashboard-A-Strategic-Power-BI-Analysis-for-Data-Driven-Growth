### Description
In this project, I created a dynamic, visually compelling Power BI dashboard using a multi-year Superstore dataset. It provides stakeholders with an intuitive platform to:

1. Track sales and profit trends (monthly & yearly)
2. Identify top-performing regions and products
3. Understand customer segments and behavior
4. Optimize discounts and marketing strategies
5. Support regional managers with KPI-focused dashboards
6. Every visual was built with clarity, interactivity, and executive usability in mind.

### Dataset Summary
The dataset spans:
- Orders: Sales, Profit, Discount, Quantity, Order Dates
- Customers: Demographics, Segments, Lifetime Value
- Products: Categories, Sub-Categories
- Geography: City, State, Region
- Returns: Tracked by Order ID
- Manager Mapping: Regional oversight and accountability

### Business Questions Answered
1. How do monthly and yearly sales trends evolve?
2. What products and regions drive the most profit?
3. Where is discounting hurting profitability?
4. Who are our most valuable customers?
5. What customer segment offers the highest margins?
6. Which states are underperforming or growing fastest?
7. How do delivery times affect performance?
8. How can we better target inventory and marketing efforts?

### Key Insights
- Q4 is the highest-performing quarter, particularly in November.
- Technology and Furniture drive the most profit.
- Excessive discounting leads to net losses in low-margin sub-categories.
- Corporate customers return fewer products and yield higher profit per sale.
- West and East regions outperform others, but opportunities exist elsewhere.
- Specific customers repeatedly drive high revenue — reward them.

### Strategic Recommendations
1. Focus marketing in West/East regions and profitable cities like New York & LA.
2. Reduce deep discounting in underperforming categories.
3. Launch loyalty campaigns for high-LTV customers.
4. Optimize delivery processing time in slower states.
5. Prepare stock ahead of high-growth months like November.
